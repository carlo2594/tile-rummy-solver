Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Summary

Tile Rummy Solver (tilerummysolver.com) has no accounts and asks for no personal information. The solver runs entirely in your browser.

What is stored on your device

To save you from re-entering a board, the solver keeps your last table, rack and settings in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can remove it at any time with the Start over button or by clearing your browser's site data.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Because of this:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalized advertising in Google's Ad Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used, and can change their choice at any time.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes basic technical data (such as IP addresses) to deliver pages and protect against abuse. The site uses your device's built-in fonts and loads no third-party fonts.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. This data is anonymous and is not used to identify you.

Tips and payments

If you send a tip, the payment is handled by the tip service (such as Ko-fi). We don't see or store your card details. That service's own privacy policy applies.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too.
